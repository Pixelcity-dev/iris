# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Iris is a security tool used by people with very different backgrounds. We want
the issue tracker, pull requests, and discussions to be a place where anyone can
report a bug or propose a rule without being talked down to.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until they prove otherwise.
- Critique the code, the rule, or the evidence — not the person.
- Keep reports reproducible. "It doesn't work" is a starting point, not a bug
  report; help each other turn it into one.
- Respect disclosure norms: no posting other people's credentials, customer
  data, or unpatched vulnerabilities in public threads.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, fake benchmarks, or fabricated scan
  results.
- Spamming issues or pull requests with promotional content.
- Encouraging or assisting attacks on systems the person does not own.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and any private communication made in the context of the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **service@pixelcity.dev**. Maintainers will review reports
confidentially and respond within five business days. Responses range from a
private warning, to removing content, to a temporary or permanent ban from the
project spaces. Maintainers who do not follow this policy may be removed from
their role.

Good-faith reports about the conduct policy itself are always welcome.
